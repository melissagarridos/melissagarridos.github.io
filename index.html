E-Commerce Sales & Profitability Analysis

End-to-end analytics project examining three years of e-commerce order data to find where the store actually makes money and where it quietly loses it — covering data cleaning, exploratory analysis in Python, SQL queries against a relational warehouse, and margin/discount modeling.

  • Electronics drives ~45% of revenue but the thinnest margin once discounting is factored in
  • Profit margin declines steadily as discount rate increases, turning net-negative past ~30%
  • Reproducible workflow with SQL warehouse queries and a full Jupyter write-up

RFM Customer Segmentation

Segments a retail customer base using Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM) analysis to answer a core retention question: who to protect, who to win back, and who isn't worth chasing — validated against unsupervised K-Means clustering.

  • Rule-based RFM scoring across 3,250 customers and 28K+ transactions
  • Named business segments: Champions, Loyal, At-Risk, Can't-Lose-Them, and more
  • K-Means clustering used as an independent validation of the rule-based segments
